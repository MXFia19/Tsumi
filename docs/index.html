Tsumi

Watch your anime and shows, alone or with friends using Watch Together.

Looking for the latest version…

Windows

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). The app updates itself when a new version is out.

Download for Windows

The installer is not code-signed: if Windows SmartScreen shows a warning, click More info, then Run anyway.

iPhone and iPad

iOS 17 or later. The app is not on the App Store: install it with a sideloading app.

With Feather (recommended)

  1. Add the Tsumi source to Feather: tap the button, or in Feather go to Sources, tap + and paste the URL.
  2. Open the source and install Tsumi. Updates show up in the same place.
Add to Feather
https://mxfia19.github.io/Tsumi/source.json

Also works with AltStore and SideStore.

With Sideloadly (from a computer)

  1. Download Tsumi.ipa on your computer.
  2. Open Sideloadly, connect your iPhone, drop the .ipa on it and sign in with your Apple ID.
  3. With a free Apple ID, the app has to be signed again every 7 days.
